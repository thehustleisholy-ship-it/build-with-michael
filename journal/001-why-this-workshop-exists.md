# 001: Why this workshop exists

I want building and learning to become part of daily life. GitHub gives this work a place where the explanation, the changes, and the next question can stay together.

This workshop follows my learning in Linux and systems, then connects those skills to practical tools for business and ministry.

My allegiance is to Jesus Christ. I want that to be visible in how I work: tell the truth about progress, credit other people's contributions, accept correction, and make something useful.

## The standard for an update

Each build report should explain the problem, the attempt, the evidence, the lesson, and the next question.

Some entries will be small. An accurately explained command is worth publishing. Larger entries should show enough of the process for another person to understand or reproduce the result.

## Where the work begins

The first focus is Linux fundamentals: navigating confidently, reading files carefully, understanding paths, and checking evidence before diagnosing a system.

## An opening question

What was the first computing task that made you feel you understood what your machine was doing?
