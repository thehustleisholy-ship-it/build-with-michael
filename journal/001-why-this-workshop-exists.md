# 001: Why I'm opening the workshop

I dream of using Linux the way people use Word or social media. Familiar enough that I can sit down, get to work, and know my way around.

The more I learn, the more intrigued I become. There's also some sadness about how much time passed before I took a closer look. I can't go back and start earlier. I can give the learning in front of me my attention now.

That's part of why I wanted this space.

I want to use GitHub as the place where I show up and share what I'm building. A place where the questions, the attempts, and the changes stay connected. Someone should be able to follow the conversation and then look at the thing we're talking about.

## Starting where I am

Right now, I'm working through Linux fundamentals. Commands, files, directories, paths, and the habits that help me understand what's happening.

There is plenty I still need to learn. I want to be able to explain why a command belongs in a script and what its output tells me. If I can't explain something yet, that's a useful place to spend more time.

I'll share those smaller lessons alongside the larger builds. Another person learning this may have the same question.

## What I want the work to reflect

I follow Jesus Christ. I want the way I build to reflect that: give credit, tell the truth about progress, receive correction, and consider the people who may use what I make.

THIH Systems and The Hustle Is Holy™ are part of that work. As the technical skills grow, I want to put them toward practical needs in business and ministry.

## Come learn with me

If you're starting, ask the question. If you know a better way, help me understand it. If an explanation is wrong, point it out so I can correct it.

I'm curious: what made Linux begin to feel natural to you?
