## A few terms

- **Repository:** The project folder and its recorded history.
- **README:** The introduction you see on the repository homepage.
- **Commit:** A saved change in that history.
- **Issue:** An actionable problem or improvement to track.
- **Pull request:** A proposed change someone can review.
- **Discussion:** A conversation, question, or exchange of ideas.

You do not have to be an engineer to point out an unclear explanation or describe a real problem.
